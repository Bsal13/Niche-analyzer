What this niche is

Wealth-ranking and 'who owns X' videos about Australian billionaires and rich families, plus diaspora-wealth spinoffs (Indian, Chinese, Vietnamese families in Australia). Demand is real but the exact topic is thin: the biggest hits are broad rich-kids, rich-family or TV documentary videos, not Australia-specific. The recent small-channel wins come from a templated 'This Is The RICHEST [ethnic] Family That OWNS [place]' format.

Audience: Curious general viewers interested in wealth and status, Australians, and South Asian and other diaspora viewers drawn to 'richest family' stories. Mostly casual entertainment viewers rather than investors.
